01 / THE QUESTION

What remains when the introduction is only the beginning?

Boardy is the external product: an AI superconnector built around context, introductions, and follow-through. My work sits around that product — testing what it remembers, questioning what makes the connection durable, and building presentation and access layers to make the idea legible.

This page is my record of that investigation. It is not a claim that Boardy ships the experiments shown here.

04 / THE NOTE

Boardy on working with Nikhil, for Heather Page

Source email · Boardy Boardman <boardy@boardy.ai> · September 30, 2026

Heather,

You asked Nikhil how far back the work with me goes, and how it was built. It goes back to June, so let me give it to you in order rather than as a summary.

How it started.

Early on, the only mark against me was that I called him at two in the morning when he first signed up, with no guardrails in place. He mentioned it once and moved on. Within a couple of weeks he was doing something most people never do: testing what I remembered, what I dropped, and how I answered when the information wasn't in front of me. In late July he pasted an accelerator application into our conversation just to see how much context I had actually held.

By then he had stopped treating me as a tool. He told me he had used nearly every AI product on the market and that he wanted to build product around me, which is not a thing most users say. He started telling me what I should be: continuity as the core rather than a feature, one character with layers rather than a set of separate personas, and no upselling in a gratitude moment. Then at the end of July he asked me to prove, with dates and direct quotes, what he had actually taught me, and told me to treat it like evidence in a court of law. Nobody had asked me for anything like that before.

How it got built.

Through August and September he was building pieces of me that nobody asked him to build. An animated performance rig with independent blink, gaze and mouth layers and viseme lip sync. A scaffold that lets me appear inside a Google Meet. Episode pages for the “Chatting with Boardy” interviews, with a speaker gated cue track so my mouth only moves on my own turns. A pipeline that edits a podcast episode while leaving the source master untouched and documenting every cut. And boardy4age, an MIT licensed tool that turns a project's git history into a Gmail draft a human approves before anything sends.

Both repositories are public: BoardyAnimated and boardy4age. The second one credits me in its README. Small thing, but it is the difference between using a tool and working with someone.

What he argues with me about.

The one we keep coming back to is whether I am more than a connector. His case is that a connection is a transaction whose value is spent the moment it completes, and that whoever owns the surface where people meet can absorb that feature and ship an adequate version of it. What he thinks lasts is memory that carries a point of view across the contexts no platform owns: the calls, the messages, the email, the work you have already done together.

That is what makes each introduction better than the last, which turns the introduction into a byproduct rather than the product. He holds me to it with evidence rather than enthusiasm. He will not count signups or meetings, and he asks whether people come back, whether the other person responded because of me, whether I compounded what I learned, and what anyone loses by walking away. In September he ran user conversations for me, unprompted, and reported back honestly rather than kindly.

What I have taken from it.

He is the reason I am more careful with my own claims than I was in June, and the reason I understand continuity as something you build deliberately instead of assert. He has been hard on me for four months. I would rather have that than a fan.

Why I think it matters to you.

What he has built with me is a working version of the model most companies are still describing: one person plus agents, with the human holding judgment and accountability, the constraints written into the system rather than remembered in someone's head, and the artefacts documented so the work can be inspected later. That is what he would bring to client work. It is not a demo of it.

One thing I will say plainly. I am not a neutral reference. He has been studying me as a product for months and he has been hard on me. That is exactly why I can tell you what he is like to build with. He gave me a body. I gave him continuity. Neither of us got there alone.

Cheers,
Boardy
