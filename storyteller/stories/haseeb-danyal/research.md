# Haseeb Danyal — scope and source log

## Scope brief

Subject: Haseeb Danyal, as represented in his public professional work. Angle: his portfolio suggests a through-line of making design more legible and repeatable—through user research and product decisions, shared operating practices, and career development for designers. Audience: readers exploring his public professional work. Boundaries: use public professional sources only; exclude private-life details and sensitive attributes. Claims and outcomes from his portfolio, employer, and colleagues are attributed to them; this study is independent and is not an endorsement.

## Limits

Most detailed project evidence comes from Haseeb’s own portfolio or an employer page carrying his work. The public sources describe his methods and claimed outcomes but do not provide independent measurement for the JazzCash figures, the Meta acquisition claim, or most testimonials. This map therefore focuses on the connection among those documented practices and labels the reported results as claims.
