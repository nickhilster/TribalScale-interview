03Core answer

How I design

Understand why → map system → prototype → humans → iterate.

  • Users + business outcome + constraints first
  • Expose the highest-risk assumption
  • Build the smallest test that can change the decision
  • Design + engineering together, not sequential handoff
  • Measure what 'working' means before scaling
04AI-native

How I use AI

Capability follows the problem. Human authority follows consequence.

  • Separate deterministic behaviour from generative behaviour
  • Use agents where they change the workflow, not merely speed up a step
  • Guardrails + auditability + human review where consequences matter
  • Evaluate the outcome, not whether the chatbot produced text
  • Example: LTB Buddy or RBH
